Internot AI

Download once. Use it with no internet.

An AI assistant, street maps with directions, phone-to-phone chat and a document reader that all run on the phone. Get the files here once over Wi‑Fi, or from a friend’s phone, and you never need data for them again.

This page will move to internot.maclesotho.com.

1. Get the app

Android phone

  1. Download the light app above (best on low data).
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installing from this source.
  3. Open Internot AI → Setup → Get maps and models.

iPhone, iPad or computer

  1. Open the browser version above once while online.
  2. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Computer: install it from the address bar.
  3. It then opens with no internet. Add maps and a model under Setup.

2. Choose an AI model for your phone

Bigger models answer more accurately but need more memory and are slower. Not sure how much memory your phone has? The app tells you under Setup → Get maps and models and marks the one that suits it.

The AI can still be wrong. Check anything important. All three models are free to use, including commercially (Apache 2.0 licence). They are downloaded from Hugging Face.

3. Country maps

Every street, village, clinic and school, with turn-by-turn directions. The Lesotho map also shows over a million house outlines.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License. Another country? Download its .osm.pbf file from download.geofabrik.de and add it in the app under Setup → Maps.

4. Put a file into the app

  1. Easiest: in the app, Setup → Get maps and models → Get. A download that stops carries on from where it stopped.
  2. From this page, a memory card, a USB stick or WhatsApp: in the app, Setup → Import a file, then choose the .imap map or .gguf model.

5. Share with no data

Quick Share or Bluetooth

In the app: Setup → Share with no data → Share the app, Share this map or Share the AI model, then pick Quick Share (Nearby Share) or Bluetooth. Quick Share is fast; Bluetooth is slow for big files.

Straight between two Internot phones

On both phones open Chat → Find nearby phones. Then Setup → Share with no data → Send to a linked phone. The other phone is asked before anything is sent.

Shops and offices

A laptop with these files can copy them to phones by USB cable or memory card, with no internet at all.

Check a file

The app checks every download itself. If you copy files another way and want to check them on a computer, each file’s SHA‑256 is listed above. On Windows: certutil -hashfile FILE SHA256. On Mac or Linux: shasum -a 256 FILE.
